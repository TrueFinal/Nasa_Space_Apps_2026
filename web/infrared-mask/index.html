How the mask works

The CWAVE band center must fall within the selected window. Invalid pixels and instrumental flags are excluded. Gaia DR3 flags nearby stars; SOURCE and OUTLIER remain visible for review.

CBAND width remains the physical response of each pixel. The selection does not create a monochromatic image or confirm an object.
